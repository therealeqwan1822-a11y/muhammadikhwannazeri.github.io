About Me

I am an engineering student at Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) specializing in digital systems, computer architecture, and embedded AI. My core technical expertise bridges lower-level hardware synthesis with edge software integration—ranging from synthesizing hardware logic gates and Finite State Machines (FSMs) on Cyclone IV FPGAs to engineering low-latency computer vision pipelines and asynchronous microcontrollers over MQTT.

Driven by end-to-end hardware validation, I focus on building functional prototypes verified across simulation testbenches (ModelSim), logic viewers (Quartus Prime), and physical bench testing setups.
